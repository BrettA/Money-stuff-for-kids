Tice Brown, Who Is a Guy

At Money Stuff, the microphones stood by; the guest was Tice Brown, who is a guy. They praised a good email, then chased the DAT craze, and gambling proxies in roundabout ways.

They tackled leverage kept in its lane: if one collateral pool suffers a strain, it cannot grab assets that back loan two— “non-cross-collateralized” says what it can’t do.

Calls nearing expiry were rolled into new, so an options position could keep going through. A dinosaur fossil came up in the trade, then art, where bid/ask gaps leave deals unmade.

The bid is the buyer’s; the ask is the price the seller will take—and they may not match nice. Odd bonds were defeased: safe assets set by to make promised payments as due dates went by.

They covered windfall-based prices and names for lawyers versus attorneys, plus regulatory claims: Nebraska insurance and Arkansas banks, then SpaceX, Tesla, and Nasdaq joined ranks.
