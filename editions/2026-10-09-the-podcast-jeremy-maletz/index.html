From Goat Herders to Data Centers

The Money Stuff microphones blinked on bright; Jeremy Maletz joined the host that night. At Susquehanna International Group, he leads Prediction-market trading on outcomes and deeds.

An event contract’s value depends on what comes true; Margin is collateral that helps payments come through. They talked about custom-made trades and market flow, And how adverse selection means someone may know.

Could election-linked factors be hedged with a trade? Could risks that move together be measured and weighed? They started with sports bets and goat-herder risk— Then bounded to data centers, startlingly brisk.

Soon trillion-dollar credit derivatives were in sight: Contracts shifting lending risk from left to right. From goats to elections to servers in rows— That is quite a journey for where finance goes!

The episode is offered through Bloomberg’s Money Stuff, On Apple and Spotify, with all that wonky stuff. Listeners can hear how far one discussion can roam: From a goat herder’s hedge to a vast server home.

What happened? The Money Stuff podcast interviewed Jeremy Maletz, Susquehanna International Group’s head of prediction markets, about event contracts, margin, hedging, adverse selection, correlation, and the leap from sports gambling to trillion-dollar data-center credit derivatives.
