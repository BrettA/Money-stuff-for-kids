One Hundred John Does and a Microphone

Late summer lingered; nostalgia drew near, when Money Stuff’s new podcast arrived for your ear. Silvia Killingsworth guest-hosted once more, with a bundle of curious questions to explore.

There were Susquehanna’s 100 John Does— a hundred unknowns in astonishing rows. Then options market makers entered the chat, buying and selling contracts this way and that.

An option gives someone a choice for a price; a market maker helps trades happen quickly and nice. To hedge all that risk, the maker can trade an offsetting position, so danger may fade.

They paced at the frontier, both nervous and slow, and asked if AI training should ease up and grow less quickly—for economic good, or so humans survive. Then: Who writes about AI when AI starts to write?

They discussed how money can feel near yet stay stuck, then pensions and 401(k)s entered their luck. The episode reached Apple and Spotify too, with many strange questions packed into one queue.

What happened? Money Stuff released a podcast episode with guest co-host Silvia Killingsworth covering Susquehanna’s 100 John Does, options-market-making hedges, slower AI training, psychological liquidity mismatches, and putting pensions back into 401(k)s. It was distributed through Apple, Spotify, and other podcast services.
