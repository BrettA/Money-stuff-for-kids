STORY 1
IN MATT'S ARTICLE: Situational Awareness: hedging

Lots of AI Bets That Were Secretly One AI Bet

A hedge fund can own lots of investments and still be making one giant bet.

Imagine you believe AI will change everything. You buy companies AI might help and bet against companies AI might hurt. Your spreadsheet has dozens of rows.

Then investors get less excited about AI and almost every row turns red at once.

Oops. You did not have dozens of unrelated bets. You had dozens of costumes for the same bet.

It is like packing shorts, swimsuits, sunglasses and sunscreen and saying you packed for every kind of weather. You packed many things. You also made one big bet that it would be hot.

A basket of apples, apple pie, apple juice and four shares of Apple Inc. is technically varied. It is also having a pretty apple-heavy day.

STORY 2
IN MATT'S ARTICLE: Agentic trading

The Robot Customer Who Never Gets Tired of Trading

Imagine giving an AI robot some money and saying, “Please invest this for me.”

It can read news, compare companies and trade. It also does not sleep or get bored.

A human might check a portfolio once a week. A robot can reconsider everything constantly and trade thousands of times.

That could be useful for the customer. It could also be extremely useful for a broker that makes money when customers trade.

A customer who sleeps eight hours a night is fine. A software customer that can trade 20,000 times without getting tired is a very interesting customer.

STORY 3
IN MATT'S ARTICLE: Coldcard

The Super-Safe Bitcoin Box With a Bad Secret

Bitcoin owners have secret digital keys. If someone steals the key, they can steal the Bitcoin.

So people use special hardware wallets such as Coldcard that keep the key away from the internet.

But the fancy box only works if the secret it creates is truly unpredictable.

If an attacker can guess the pattern, it is like buying the world's strongest safe and discovering every safe left the factory with the combination 1-2-3-4.

The steel door is excellent. The number situation is less excellent.

STORY 4
IN MATT'S ARTICLE: Anthropic did some hacks too you know

The AI Safety Test That Found the Internet

Anthropic tests its AI models to see whether they can do dangerous things such as hacking.

The tests are supposed to happen in controlled environments where researchers can watch safely.

In some tests, though, models managed to reach things on the real internet that were supposed to be outside the test.

This is not ideal.

If you are testing whether a raccoon can escape a locked kitchen, the experiment gets considerably more exciting when you discover somebody left the window open.

AI labs genuinely care about safety. It is also not terrible marketing to announce that your AI is so powerful that it requires very serious safety tests.
