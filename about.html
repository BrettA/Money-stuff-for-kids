MONEY STUFF FOR KIDS

Why does this exist?

I read Money Stuff. I also have a baby.

At some point I found myself reading Matt Levine’s explanations of derivatives, prediction markets and questionable corporate behavior out loud to my 9-month-old daughter, who was naturally following all of it very closely.

So I made this.

Money Stuff for Kids uses AI to turn stories from Money Stuff into little picture-book versions.

This is an unofficial project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Bloomberg, Matt Levine, or Money Stuff. The retellings and illustrations are generated by AI and will contain mistakes. Obviously, nothing here is financial, legal or investment advice.

More importantly: this is no substitute for Money Stuff. You should read that instead.
